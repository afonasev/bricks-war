# Quiet Mechanism tactile clips

Source: **Bub Block Sound Effects** by roppychop / Roppy Chop Studios.
Recorded source files: pickup, open, close, drop1, drop2, drop3.
Source URL and CC0 1.0 license are recorded in `../realistic-sfx/LICENSE.md`.

These six derivatives preserve the exact PCM of the isolated rotate/placement
events in the user-selected audition
`audio-previews/piece-sounds-v1/02-quiet-mechanism.wav` (choice 2, 2026-10-02).
Processing removes leading silence, trims duration, fades cut edges, applies
lowpass and normalizes peak. Audition gain is baked into the files. Playback
rate is 1; no additional oscillator layer or runtime filter is used.
Exact segment times, frame counts and hashes are in `provenance.json`.
